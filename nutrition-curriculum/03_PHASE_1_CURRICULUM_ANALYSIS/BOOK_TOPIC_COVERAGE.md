# Book Topic Coverage Matrix

Ratings are based **only** on what each book's TOC entry implies about depth of treatment — not on assumed content. `P` = a dedicated chapter (or the clear majority of one) is devoted to the topic. `S` = a substantial section within a chapter (typically several TOC sub-entries). `M` = a minor subsection, single bullet, or passing appendix mention. `—` = not represented in that book's TOC at all. Where the TOC entry's depth was ambiguous, the **more conservative** rating was used (per Task 5's instruction).

## Notes on Method

- Every `P` in this table corresponds to a full or near-full chapter (or, for AS3/ACSM, a full appendix cluster) named explicitly in that book's TOC — never inferred.
- Where a topic spans multiple chapters of one book (e.g., KM16's disease chapters each rated `P` individually rather than pooled), each chapter was rated on its own terms in `MASTER_TOPIC_UNIVERSE.md`'s provenance; this matrix reflects the topic as a whole.
- The **CLIN domain is almost a monoculture** (KM16 `P` in all 27 rows, everything else mostly `—`) — this is a direct, honest reflection of the TOCs, not an error. It is analyzed explicitly in `BOOK_ROLES.md` and `TOPIC_OVERLAPS.md`.
- The **RESEARCH domain** is the mirror image — NRM `P` in all 15 rows, everyone else mostly `—`.
- A `—` does not mean the book's actual text lacks the topic — only that its own Table of Contents does not name it as a distinguishable heading. This distinction is discussed further in `APPARENT_CURRICULUM_GAPS.md`.
