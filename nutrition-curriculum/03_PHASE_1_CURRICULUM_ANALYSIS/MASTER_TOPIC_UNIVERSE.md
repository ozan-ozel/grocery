## How to Read This Document

- Each **Domain** (e.g. `NUT`, `MET`) is a broad knowledge area.
- Each **Topic** (`NUT-01`) is a meaningful, book-spanning subject.
- Each **Subtopic** (`NUT-01.01`) is a meaningful subdivision preserved because at least one book treats it distinctly.
- **Sources** lines give provenance: book code, the chapter/section the topic came from, and — where useful — the TOC's own wording in quotes. A topic with only one source book is still a real topic; single-sourcing is exactly what `APPARENT_CURRICULUM_GAPS.md` and `BOOK_ROLES.md` analyze separately.
- P/S/M coverage ratings are **not** repeated here — they live in `BOOK_TOPIC_COVERAGE.md` to avoid duplicating judgment calls in two places. This file's job is the taxonomy and provenance; the coverage file's job is the strength rating.
- KM16's own internal chapter structure (Etiology → Pathophysiology → Medical Management → Medical Nutrition Therapy) repeats almost identically across ~35 of its disease-specific chapters. That structural template is not listed as a separate topic under every disease topic below — it is simply how KM16 organizes each disease topic's content, noted once here rather than 35 times.
