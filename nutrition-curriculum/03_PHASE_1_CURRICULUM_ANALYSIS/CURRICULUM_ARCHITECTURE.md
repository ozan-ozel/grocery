## Why This Shape, Not the Example Shape

The Task 3 prompt offered an 18-category example list as a starting point. The structure above keeps that same category count (18 domains) but reshapes several of them because the seven books, taken together, justify a different split than a generic nutrition curriculum would:

1. **Dietary Reference Values got its own domain (DRV)** rather than folding into Foundations, because three different books (ACSM, KM16, and to a lesser extent AS3) each carry substantial standalone DRI-appendix content that functions as reference material, not narrative teaching — it behaves differently enough from NUT's prose-based content to warrant separation.

2. **Clinical Nutrition (CLIN) is enormous and single-book-dominated** in a way no generic curriculum outline would predict — 27 of its 45 possible chapter-level topics trace to KM16 alone. The architecture reflects this honestly rather than padding CLIN with thinner cross-references it doesn't actually have, per `APPARENT_CURRICULUM_GAPS.md`.

3. **Research Methods (RESEARCH) is equally single-book-dominated** (NRM alone) — kept as its own top-level domain rather than merged into Foundations or Assessment, because its content (study design, statistics, omics, biobanks) is categorically different from what any other domain contains.

4. **GI Physiology got its own domain** rather than folding into Foundations or Metabolism, because five different books treat it at chapter length with a clearly identifiable subject boundary (digestion/absorption mechanics, distinct from macronutrient metabolism *after* absorption).

5. **A `SPECIAL` catch-all domain was kept small and explicitly cross-referencing**, rather than eliminated — nutritional genomics, complementary/integrative medicine, counseling, and cultural competency each appear in exactly one book (KM16) but don't fit cleanly inside any of the nutrient- or disease-organized domains; forcing them into CLIN or NUT would have been the "unnatural classification" the task instructions warned against.

6. **Life Course (LIFE) is real and entirely KM16-sourced** — kept as its own domain (matching the Task 3 example) rather than merged into CLIN, because pregnancy/infancy/childhood/adolescence/aging are physiological life stages, not diseases, even though only one book chapters them this way.

No domain from the Task 3 example list was dropped outright; "Special Topics" survives as `SPECIAL`, "Public Health / Population Nutrition" survives as `PUBHEALTH`, and the macronutrient/micronutrient split survives intact. The main structural deviations are the DRV split-out and the internal organ-system grouping imposed on CLIN's 27 topics (grouped here into 8 clusters purely for this diagram's readability — `MASTER_TOPIC_UNIVERSE.md` keeps them as 27 flat, individually-IDed topics, since no book's TOC actually groups them this way itself).
