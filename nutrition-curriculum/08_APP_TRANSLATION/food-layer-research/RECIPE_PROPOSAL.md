# Meal components and core recipe proposal

## Scope and quantity convention

These are **24 proposed ingredient-list preparations**, not finalized recipes or nutritional prescriptions. All mandatory food ingredients are already in the audited 89-row catalog. Optional future additions are explicitly identified. No gram amounts, nutrient totals, cooking times, shelf lives, or storage temperatures are invented.

The proposals are newly composed planning examples, not reproduced recipes from source books. Cultural practicality and home-preparation suitability are design judgments for user/kitchen review. Nutritional roles describe ingredients' potential contribution; exact source matching and portions must precede claims about a prepared serving.

Use the [raw/cooked specification](README.md#f-rawcooked-weight-specification). Each recipe has a **protein component**, **starch component**, **vegetable/fruit component**, and/or **fat component** as applicable; the role is not an amount or percentage. “Adjust protein” means select a practical amount of that ingredient within later approved bounds, then recalculate all nutrients—not change protein alone. “Adjust starch” likewise changes its accompanying nutrients.

Grains and dry pulses are weighed dry for preparation or use a separately verified cooked reference. Meats specify raw edible input and cooked output if logging cooked. Eggs exclude shell; tuna specifies drained state. Yogurt/cheese/bread are their verified ready-to-eat product. Oil and salt are explicit ingredients whenever used. Water is a preparation ingredient where necessary; its final retained amount matters for yield.

Static “simple/moderate” descriptions below do not implement DEC-068 skill/time/equipment matching. “Batch-suitable” means a plausible home-prep candidate, not a validated shelf-life guarantee. Storage guidance needs an appropriate source and later review. Fresh salad components may be assembled separately at serving.

## H. Standard component library

| Component structure | Existing food choices | Macro-completion flexibility | Practical boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lean poultry + starch + vegetable | Chicken/turkey; rice/bulgur/potato; broccoli/cauliflower | Protein and starch adjusted separately; oil separately | Do not make cooked/raw assumptions |
| Egg + bread + vegetables | Egg, whole-wheat bread, tomato/pepper | Bread adjusts starch; egg changes protein and fat together | Egg count needs edible mass; oil accounted |
| Yogurt + oats + fruit | Yogurt, oats, banana/apple/strawberry | Oats/fruit add carbs; yogurt adds mixed macros | Ordinary yogurt is not a lean protein isolate |
| Pulses + grain + vegetables | Lentils/chickpeas/beans; bulgur/rice | Both pulse and grain contribute carbs; pulses add protein/fiber | Not unrestricted carb-free protein completion |
| Mince + potato/pasta + vegetables | Defined mince fat variant; potato/pasta; tomato/carrot | Protein/fat linked to mince choice; starch separate | Fat variant and drippings matter |
| Fish + grain/tuber + vegetables | Salmon/tuna; potato/rice; greens | Lean versus fatty fish is an intentional choice | Protein-equivalent fish need not be omega-3-equivalent |
| Grain + pulse salad | Buckwheat/quinoa/bulgur + chickpeas | Plant protein/carbs with separately measured oil | Cooked pulse/grain basis and exclusions |
| Vegetable + yogurt side | Spinach/cucumber/zucchini + yogurt | Vegetable/calcium-oriented addition | Usually a component, not complete meal |
| Nut + fruit snack | Walnut/almond + apple/raisins | Compact fat/carbs, some protein/fiber | Not a lean-protein recommendation |
| Measured dressing | Olive oil or tahini + lemon/water | Fat adjustment and culinary cohesion | Tahini has extra macros and sesame allergen |

A template is a permitted role structure. A recipe chooses ingredients and a preparation concept. A nutrient profile belongs to a food in a state, and a serving is a measured amount of prepared food. These objects must remain distinguishable.

## I. Twenty-four proposals

Nutrition contribution codes used below are qualitative, not adequacy labels:
**Ca** calcium; **Fe** iron; **Fol** folate; **Mg** magnesium; **K** potassium;
**C** vitamin C; **A** carotenoid/vitamin-A contribution; **B12** vitamin B12;
**D** vitamin D; **ω3** fatty-acid contribution (not a micronutrient).
Food-group evidence is summarized in Sources. Every recipe still needs a qualified composition profile.

### 1. Tavuklu pirinç ve brokoli

- **Ingredients/quantity concept:** `tavuk göğsü` as protein portion; `pirinç` as dry starch portion; `brokoli` as vegetable portion; measured `zeytinyağı`; `su`, `tuz` if used.
- **Role:** Balanced, protein-adjustable lunch/dinner. Broccoli contributes potential C/Fol; poultry contributes protein and B vitamins.
- **Preparation:** Simple separate cooked components; batch candidate with state/yield measurements.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** `hindi göğsü` for chicken, `bulgur` for rice subject to gluten restrictions; `karnabahar` as cooler-season vegetable alternative.
- **Scaling:** Adjust poultry/starch separately; record oil. Reuse existing combo identity `tavuk-pirinc-brokoli` if this is selected as its reviewed evolution; existing grams are not validated servings.

### 2. Tavuklu bulgur ve kabak

- **Ingredients:** `tavuk göğsü`, dry `bulgur`, `kabak`, `domates`, `soğan`, measured `zeytinyağı`, `su`, optional `tuz`.
- **Role:** Balanced lunch/dinner; bulgur supplies starch/fiber, vegetables add dietary-quality roles.
- **Preparation:** Simple poultry plus vegetable bulgur; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Turkey; rice if gluten exclusion permits only non-bulgur option; cauliflower/broccoli instead of summer zucchini with changed preparation.
- **Scaling:** Poultry and dry bulgur independently; water changes with actual method rather than fixed multiplication of cooked grams.

### 3. Hindili sebzeli makarna

- **Ingredients:** `hindi göğsü`, dry `makarna`, `domates`, `mantar`, `sarımsak`, `zeytinyağı`, `su`, optional `tuz`.
- **Role:** Protein/carbohydrate lunch/dinner; tomato and mushrooms contribute complementary vegetable roles.
- **Preparation:** Simple sauce plus pasta; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Chicken; zucchini for mushrooms as a culinary variant; winter frozen tomato requires an explicit sourced state.
- **Scaling:** Adjust poultry and pasta; track sauce/oil contribution and drained pasta yield.

### 4. Tavuklu patates tepsisi

- **Ingredients:** `tavuk göğsü`, `patates`, `havuç`, `soğan`, measured `zeytinyağı`, optional `tuz`.
- **Role:** Balanced lunch/dinner; potato K, carrot A potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple tray concept; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Turkey; zucchini/cauliflower for part of vegetable component; keep potato as starch.
- **Scaling:** Poultry and potato separately. Whole-tray weight cannot substitute for separately portioning meat and potato.

### 5. Somon, patates ve ıspanak

- **Ingredients:** `somon`, `patates`, `ıspanak`; measured `zeytinyağı` if used; optional `tuz`.
- **Role:** Protein/fat-oriented balanced dinner; fish B12/D/ω3 potential; potato K, spinach Fol.
- **Preparation:** Simple separate components; batch/storage suitability requires review.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Broccoli for spinach; optional future sardine species/product, with recalculation. Tuna changes oily-fish role.
- **Scaling:** Potato controls starch; fish changes protein/fat together. Existing combo `somon-patates-ispanak` is a candidate predecessor.

### 6. Somonlu pirinç ve karnabahar

- **Ingredients:** `somon`, dry `pirinç`, `karnabahar`, `limon`, `su`; oil/salt only if explicitly included.
- **Role:** Balanced main meal; salmon B12/D/ω3, cauliflower vegetable/C role.
- **Preparation:** Simple fish + rice + vegetable; batch candidate subject to storage validation.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Broccoli; rice→bulgur with exclusion review; fish alternatives re-evaluate fats.
- **Scaling:** Rice changes starch; fish changes protein/fat; lemon is seasoning, not a fruit-sized vitamin-C prescription.

### 7. Ton balıklı nohut salatası

- **Ingredients:** drained `ton balığı`, cooked/drained `nohut`, `domates`, `salatalık`, `limon`, measured `zeytinyağı`.
- **Role:** Protein-focused lunch with pulse carbohydrate/fiber; B12/Fol/Fe potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple assembly using separately prepared pulses; keep fresh components separate for prep.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Chicken if fish excluded; chickpea→beans; cooler-season carrot replaces some salad texture, not identical nutrients.
- **Scaling:** Tuna and chickpeas independently; confirm tuna species/product and fish guidance before repeated-default recommendation.

### 8. Karidesli kabak ve mantar

- **Ingredients:** `karides`, `kabak`, `mantar`, measured `zeytinyağı`, `sarımsak`; optional `pirinç` as separate starch component.
- **Role:** Protein-focused low-starch component; optional rice makes a fuller main meal. Seafood B12 potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple sauté concept; fresh preparation preferred as a design choice, not a safety rule.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Chicken for shrimp after exclusion checks; broccoli for zucchini with method change.
- **Scaling:** Protein portion first, rice independently if needed; measure retained cooking fat. Existing `karides-kabak-mantar` may be evolved.

### 9. Dana kıymalı domatesli makarna

- **Ingredients:** defined `dana kıyma` variant, dry `makarna`, `domates`, `soğan`, `sarımsak`, `su`; oil/salt if used.
- **Role:** Balanced main; meat Fe/zinc/B12 potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple sauce; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Verified other mince fat variant is a recalculated variant; lentil sauce is a different macro pattern, not equal-weight meat replacement.
- **Scaling:** Pasta and mince independently; preserve fat/drippings assumptions. Candidate existing ID `kiyma-makarna-domates`.

### 10. Kıymalı patates yemeği

- **Ingredients:** `dana kıyma`, `patates`, `domates`, `soğan`, `su`, measured `zeytinyağı`, optional `tuz`.
- **Role:** Balanced main; Fe/B12 plus potato K.
- **Preparation:** Simple stew; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Carrot alongside potato; optional future salça in a separately recalculated version.
- **Scaling:** Meat and potato amounts; final liquid yield for serving allocation, without pretending every ladle is compositionally identical.

### 11. Kuzu etli sebzeli bulgur

- **Ingredients:** `kuzu but` edible meat, dry `bulgur`, `havuç`, `soğan`, `domates`, `su`; measured oil if used.
- **Role:** Protein/starch main with higher inherent fat depending on cut; Fe/zinc/B12 and carotenoid potential.
- **Preparation:** Moderate stew/grain concept; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Defined mince or chicken changes preparation and macros; winter carrot to summer zucchini for vegetable role.
- **Scaling:** Grain and meat separately; cut trimming/retained fat must be established.

### 12. Yumurtalı tam buğday kahvaltısı

- **Ingredients:** shell-free `yumurta`, `tam buğday ekmeği`, `domates`, `salatalık`; cooking oil only if used.
- **Role:** Balanced breakfast; egg B12/choline and grain fiber potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple egg plus bread and fresh vegetables.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Pepper/carrot vegetable variants; bread type requires source/allergen review.
- **Scaling:** Egg changes protein/fat; bread changes starch. Preserve candidate ID `yumurta-tam-bugday-domates` if selected.

### 13. Menemen ve ekmek

- **Ingredients:** `yumurta`, `domates`, `yeşil biber`, measured `zeytinyağı`, optional `soğan`, `tam buğday ekmeği`.
- **Role:** Balanced breakfast/light main; eggs B12/choline, vegetables C/carotenoids.
- **Preparation:** Simple pan dish; fresh assembly or separately prepared vegetable base.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Winter preserved tomato state needs its own composition; no silent ketchup substitution.
- **Scaling:** Egg and bread separately; vegetable-base amount must remain culinary feasible.

### 14. Ispanaklı yumurta

- **Ingredients:** `yumurta`, `ıspanak`, `soğan`, measured `zeytinyağı`; optional `tam buğday ekmeği`.
- **Role:** Protein/vegetable component; optional bread supplies starch. Fol/carotenoid/B12 potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple cooked greens and egg.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Mushroom or verified karalahana variant; changes nutrient and cooking profile.
- **Scaling:** Egg count-to-mass and bread portion; spinach is not an equal-calcium dairy substitute.

### 15. Beyaz peynirli nohut kasesi

- **Ingredients:** verified `beyaz peynir`, cooked `nohut`, `domates`, `salatalık`, `limon`; measured oil if used.
- **Role:** Balanced vegetarian lunch; calcium potential from cheese, Fol/Fe from chickpea; sodium unresolved.
- **Preparation:** Simple assembly; cooked pulse can be prepared ahead.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Yogurt changes texture and macros; winter carrot/leafy option.
- **Scaling:** Chickpea and cheese independently; do not increase salty cheese solely to chase protein.

### 16. Yoğurtlu yulaf ve muz

- **Ingredients:** `yoğurt`, `yulaf ezmesi`, `muz`; optional measured `ceviz`.
- **Role:** Carbohydrate-forward mixed breakfast/snack; calcium/Mg/K potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple bowl, no added sugar required as concept.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Apple/pear in cooler seasons or strawberry seasonally; not potassium-equivalent automatically.
- **Scaling:** Oats and yogurt independently; optional walnut changes fat. Strained yogurt is a future verified variant, not current alias reuse.

### 17. Sütlü elmalı yulaf

- **Ingredients:** `süt`, `yulaf ezmesi`, `elma`; optional measured `badem`; `su` if used.
- **Role:** Carbohydrate/calcium breakfast; fiber/Mg contribution.
- **Preparation:** Simple porridge; measured final yield if stored/portioned.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Pear, summer peach; conditional fortified-soy product for dairy-free variant.
- **Scaling:** Oats and milk; no vitamin-D credit from current US milk proxy until local fortification verified.

### 18. Kırmızı mercimek çorbası

- **Ingredients:** dry `kırmızı mercimek`, `soğan`, `havuç`, `su`, measured `zeytinyağı`, optional `tuz`.
- **Role:** Pulse carbohydrate/protein/fiber component; Fol/Fe and carrot A potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple soup; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Vegetables may vary; green lentil changes texture and recipe; optional salça future variant.
- **Scaling:** Dry lentil portion versus added water is distinct; dilution changes per-100-g soup, not batch nutrient total. Existing `mercimek-corbasi` is candidate predecessor.

### 19. Nohutlu bulgur pilavı

- **Ingredients:** cooked `nohut` or documented dry-preparation route; dry `bulgur`, `domates`, `soğan`, `su`, measured `zeytinyağı`.
- **Role:** Plant-protein/carbohydrate main component; Fol/Fe/Mg potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple once pulses cooked; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Beans; rice for bulgur with allergy review; season-specific vegetables as optional separate side.
- **Scaling:** Pulses and grain jointly evaluated because both contribute carbohydrate; not an isolated protein completion food.

### 20. Kuru fasulye ve pirinç

- **Ingredients:** `kuru fasulye` with verified species and dry/cooked route; dry `pirinç`, `soğan`, `domates`, `su`, measured `zeytinyağı`.
- **Role:** Plant-protein/carbohydrate main; Fol/Fe/fiber potential.
- **Preparation:** Moderate due to pulse preparation; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Chickpeas; bulgur with exclusion check; winter preserved tomato requires state matching.
- **Scaling:** Bean/grain proportions recalculated; soaking time is not erased by a “quick” label. Existing `kuru-fasulye-pirinc` is candidate predecessor.

### 21. Nohutlu karabuğday salatası

- **Ingredients:** dry `karabuğday`, cooked `nohut`, `domates`, `salatalık`, `limon`, measured `zeytinyağı`.
- **Role:** Plant-protein/carbohydrate lunch; Mg/Fol/fiber potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple with pre-cooked components; assemble vegetables at serving.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Quinoa; carrot/greens in cooler season; gluten-free eligibility must be verified, not inferred from ingredient name.
- **Scaling:** Grain and pulse amounts; fresh vegetables and dressing separately.

### 22. Sebzeli kinoa ve yoğurt

- **Ingredients:** dry `kinoa`, `kabak`, `havuç`, `yoğurt`, `su`, measured `zeytinyağı`.
- **Role:** Mixed carbohydrate/vegetarian main; Mg, calcium, carrot A potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple cooked grain/vegetables plus yogurt side; batch candidate.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Buckwheat/bulgur; cauliflower in cooler season; dairy-free variant needs verified substitute.
- **Scaling:** Quinoa, yogurt, and oil adjust different macro mixtures; no claim quinoa alone completes protein needs.

### 23. Yoğurtlu ıspanak ve nohut

- **Ingredients:** `ıspanak`, cooked `nohut`, `yoğurt`, `sarımsak`, measured `zeytinyağı`.
- **Role:** Vegetable/pulse/protein side or light meal; Fol/Fe plus dairy calcium potential.
- **Preparation:** Simple cooked greens with separately assembled yogurt; batch components separately.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Optional future semizotu changes to a raw summer preparation; existing zucchini is a different cooked vegetable variant.
- **Scaling:** Chickpea and yogurt independently. Serve alongside an independently scaled grain if the remaining need warrants it.

### 24. Tahinli nohut ezmesi ve sebze tabağı

- **Ingredients:** cooked `nohut`, `tahin`, `limon`, `sarımsak`, `su`, `havuç`, `salatalık`; optional `tam buğday ekmeği`.
- **Role:** Plant-protein/carbohydrate/fat component or snack; Fol/Fe/Mg potential. Tahini calcium depends on product/seed processing.
- **Preparation:** Simple blended spread; portion spread and fresh sides separately.
- **Substitutions/seasons:** Beans produce a distinct spread variant; winter carrot versus summer cucumber; sesame-free variant must be explicitly reformulated and rechecked.
- **Scaling:** Chickpea changes carbs/protein; tahini changes fat and texture; bread supplies optional starch. No assumption that this is a low-fat protein food.

## Coverage and release conditions

The set includes poultry, eggs, seafood, red meat, dairy, pulses, grains, vegetables, breakfast bowls, soups/stews, and an adjustable spread. The number 24 is a proposed editorial size, not a scientifically optimal recipe count. Several share components intentionally, allowing ingredient reuse and more predictable preparation. Optional starch sides allow a protein/vegetable component to address a different remaining need without inventing a completely new recipe.

Seven existing combo IDs are explicitly identified above as possible predecessors. This is a compatibility proposal, not an ID assignment or instruction to delete the other nine combos. The recipe library must preserve existing IDs/history or provide an approved mapping.

Before publication, each selected preparation requires exact ingredient-state/profile references, approved practical quantities, included oil/water/salt, household preparation validation, yield where used, substitution limits, relevant allergy metadata, and a clear serving basis. “No added salt” or “high protein” claims require their own confirmed definition and data. Exact gram amounts remain unset.
